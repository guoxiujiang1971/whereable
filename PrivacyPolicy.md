# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 2026**

## Introduction

Whereable ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy
Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use the
Whereable app on iOS and Android (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with
this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Account information**: email address and display name when you register or sign in.
  Sign in with Apple is supported; in that case Apple provides your identity token and we
  do not store your Apple password.
- **Item data**: names, descriptions, categories, locations, purchase dates, expiry dates,
  prices, barcodes, notes, and photos you associate with your items.
- **Family information**: family members, their display names, and permission settings.
- **Avatar images**: photos you choose to generate an AI avatar with.

### Automatically Collected Information

Whereable does not collect analytics, crash reports, usage statistics, or telemetry. We
do not use any third-party analytics SDKs, crash reporting tools, or advertising
frameworks.

### Device Permissions

With your consent, the App may access:

- **Camera**: used to scan QR codes and item barcodes. We do not capture, store, or
  transmit camera data beyond what you explicitly attach to an item.
- **Photo Library**: used to select photos for your items. You control which photos are
  associated with items.
- **Notifications**: used to send local reminders for item expiry dates. No push
  notification data is collected by us.

## How We Use Your Information

- Display and organize your items
- Sync your data across your devices via your account
- Enable search and filtering
- Facilitate family sharing within the App
- Generate QR codes for moving
- Send local expiry notifications
- Provide import/export (CSV, PDF) functionality
- Generate AI avatars (only when you request it)

## Data Storage and Sync

### Local Storage

Item data is stored locally on your device using Core Data for fast offline access.

### Cloud Sync (Supabase)

When you sign in, your data is synced to and stored on Supabase servers (PostgreSQL
database and file storage buckets for item photos and avatars). Supabase is a data
processor that hosts data on our behalf. Data is protected by encryption in transit and
access is governed by row-level security tied to your account and family.

### Family Sharing

The App has a built-in family system: you can invite family members, and each member
accesses the shared family data through their own account. You control each member's
permission level. On iOS, subscriptions may additionally be shared through Apple Family
Sharing.

### Payments

- **iOS**: subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We never receive or
  store your payment card information.
- **Android**: subscriptions are processed by Paddle. We never receive or store your
  payment card information.

## Third-Party Services

- **Supabase** (supabase.com): cloud database and file storage (data processor) for both platforms.
- **AI avatar generation**:
  - **iOS**: generated with Apple's Image Playground. Images are processed by Apple; no third-party AI service is used.
  - **Android**: generated via **OpenRouter** (openrouter.ai). When you request an avatar, the image and your prompt are transmitted to OpenRouter's AI providers.
- **Payments**: **Apple** (StoreKit, iOS) and **Paddle** (Android) process subscriptions. We never receive or store your payment card information.
- **Payoneer**: used by us to receive payouts; no user data is shared with Payoneer.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties except as described
above (hosting, payments, and AI avatar generation). We do not use your data for
advertising or profiling.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on our servers and your device for as long as you use the App. You may
delete individual items at any time. To permanently delete your account and all associated
data, use **Settings → 删除账号 (Delete Account)**. This removes your account, your data,
and your storage files; this action cannot be undone.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the
App and/or on our website. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes
acceptance of the updated policy.

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal data,
including the right to access, correct, or delete your data. You can exercise these
rights directly through the App (including the Delete Account feature) or by contacting us.
