# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 20, 2026**

## Introduction

Whereable ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use the Whereable iOS application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

Whereable is a local-first item database app. The data you enter into the App is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework. This includes:

- **Item data**: names, descriptions, categories, locations, purchase dates, expiry dates, prices, barcodes, notes, and photos you associate with your items.
- **Family member information**: names and permission settings you configure for family sharing.
- **Box/container data**: organization information you create.

### Automatically Collected Information

Whereable **does not** collect any analytics, crash reports, usage statistics, or telemetry. We do not use any third-party analytics SDKs, crash reporting tools, or advertising frameworks.

### Device Permissions

With your consent, the App may access:

- **Camera**: used to scan QR codes on moving boxes and item barcodes. We do not capture, store, or transmit any camera data beyond what you explicitly choose to attach to an item.
- **Photo Library**: used to select photos for your items. You control which photos are associated with items.
- **Notifications**: used to send local reminders for item expiry dates. No push notification data is collected by us.

### Siri & Search

If you enable Siri integration, items you choose to share with Siri may be indexed on your device for Spotlight search. Apple handles all Siri data processing in accordance with its own privacy policy.

## How We Use Your Information

All data is used solely to provide the App's core functionality:

- Display and organize your items
- Enable search and filtering
- Facilitate family sharing via iCloud
- Generate QR codes for moving
- Send local expiry notifications
- Support Siri shortcuts and Spotlight search
- Provide import/export (CSV, PDF) functionality

## Data Storage and Sync

### Local Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device using Core Data.

### iCloud Sync

Whereable uses `NSPersistentCloudKitContainer` to optionally sync your data across your devices via your personal iCloud account. Data synced through iCloud is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple. Apple's Privacy Policy governs iCloud data handling.

### Family Sharing

If you enable family sharing, you invite family members via `CKShare`. Each family member accesses the shared database through their own iCloud account. You control each member's access level (read-only or read-write) on a per-item basis.

### In-App Purchases

Purchase information (product identifiers) is stored in `NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore` to sync entitlement status across your devices. Apple processes all payments through StoreKit; we never receive or store payment card information.

## Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond the App's core functionality.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device and iCloud account for as long as you use the App. You may delete individual items at any time through the App interface. To delete all data, uninstall the App. iCloud data may remain in your iCloud account until you delete it through iCloud settings.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the App and/or on our website. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete your data. Since all data is stored on your device and iCloud account, you can exercise these rights directly through the App or your Apple device settings.
